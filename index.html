Origami Rooms Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Origami Rooms is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what information the game and the services built into it collect, why, and the choices you have. The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or contacts.

Information stored by the game

  • Game progress and settings (rooms solved, best moves, Daily results and streak, Endless scores, hints, sound and control settings) are stored on your device. Story progress, Daily results and your Endless best score are also synced through your own iCloud account so they follow you to your other devices. We cannot see this data.
  • Purchases (Remove Ads, hint packs) are handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details.
  • Game Center: if you are signed in to Game Center, your Endless best score is posted to the "Endless best" leaderboard under your Game Center name. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Notifications: the daily reminder is scheduled on your device. No information is sent anywhere to deliver it.
  • Sharing: share cards and replay videos are made on your device and only leave it when you choose where to send them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's software may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking;
  • information about your interaction with ads;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • diagnostic and performance data.

The game asks for permission before tracking you across other companies' apps and websites (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt). If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised using that identifier. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy choices for ads.

See how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played and how well it is funded by ads, so we can improve it. It records events such as starting and finishing a room, using a hint, seeing an ad, ad revenue, purchases and sharing, together with a random app-instance identifier and basic device information (model, OS version, country). It does not record your name or contact details.

See Google's Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

How information is used

  • to run the game and keep your progress;
  • to show ads that keep the game free, and to limit how often they appear;
  • to understand and improve the game.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it to change the difficulty of the game: every player gets the same rooms.

Children

Origami Rooms is rated 4+ but is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of the App Store Kids category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • Tracking: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): in the game, Settings → Privacy choices for ads.
  • Remove Ads: the Remove Ads purchase turns off banner and between-room ads.
  • Delete your data: deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. iCloud-synced progress can be removed in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device and in your iCloud until you delete it. Google keeps advertising and analytics data according to its own retention policies. Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit.
